    02

    It worked. That was the problem.

    Built more than ten years ago and patched by developer after developer. We took it over as ASWIG, now SOY Agency, and kept it running. It made money, so nobody dared to touch it.

    What it felt like

    • Afraid to change prices. One rate update could break another form.
    • Every policy re-keyed into the insurer's system by hand.
    • No single view of quotes, payments or partners.
    • Small changes took weeks. Every fix had to be made in many places.
    • Four products, four journeys for customers.

    Build 1

    Just give it a new face.

    We redesigned the front end and left the engine alone. It looked modern. Every quote still ran through the same tangle.

    Dead end.

    Build 2

    Rebuild on the old code.

    We migrated the system on top of the legacy code to save time. It was faster for a while, then every old bug came back.

    Dead end.

    03

    Tear it all down.

    Blank page. Four clean flows for domestic, import/export, vehicle & personal effects and annual cover, each with its own pricing, processing and documents.

    And it worked.

    Clean, tested, safe to change. For the first time in years, the platform was in good shape.

    Build 3 was good enough to ship.

    Then the client asked for one simple journey instead of four forms. Most teams would have shipped Build 3 and talked them out of it.

    We threw a working system away and started a fourth time.

    “

    "I named the folder 'promise, this is the last time' because I needed to read it every morning."

    Thien Nguyen, Head of New Technology, SOY Agency

    The Build 4 project folder

    AAA_hua_la_lan_cuoi

    Vietnamese for "promise, this is the last time." Three A's to pin it to the top of every list, and there in the file path of every line of code we wrote.

    We kept it.
    04

    One experience. Two engines behind it.

    One form for every product. Retail and wholesale pricing kept strictly apart, so one can change without breaking the other.

    The quote form customers use today. The price updates as they type.

    From quote to insurer policy, with nobody in the loop.

    Six documents go out with every quote, and the policy lands in the insurer's system on its own.

    1. QuoteOne form, priced live
    2. Pay or acceptCard payment, or forwarders accept on account
    3. VerifyEvery payment checked against the gateway
    4. IssueConfirmation, tax invoice, certificate
    5. Insurer policy createdStraight into Steadfast INSIGHT, no re-keying

    A back office the team runs themselves.

    Every quote, payment and partner in one place, with insurer status side by side. No developer needed for day-to-day work.
